GOOD TO KNOW

Questions & answers.

A few helpful notes before your appointment.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the Contact / Book link to visit the booking page and choose the option that best fits your needs.

What if I am not sure which service to choose?

Start with a consultation or share your goals through the booking page so the right service can be discussed.

Can I bring inspiration photos?

Yes. Bringing a few reference photos can help explain the shape, color, and finish you have in mind.

How should I prepare for a color appointment?

Share your recent color history and any hair goals when booking. That context helps guide the consultation.

Where can I find appointment details or policies?

Check the booking page for the latest availability and appointment information.
