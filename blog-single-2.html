Bring your idea. Leave with a plan.

A little preparation can make it easier to talk about the look you want.

Save a few reference photos

Choose examples that show the shape, tone, or finish you like. A picture is a useful starting point, and the conversation can adapt it to your hair and routine.

Think about your everyday routine

Consider how often you like to style your hair and what feels realistic on a busy morning. That helps shape a result that is easier to wear beyond the salon.

Share your hair history

Recent color or chemical services can affect the options available. Sharing that history early helps keep the plan thoughtful and clear.
