HAIR NOTES · CARE

Small habits, better hair days.

A gentle routine can make it easier to care for your hair between appointments.

Hair-care photo to be added

Start with what your hair needs

Hair routines do not need to be complicated. A few consistent steps, chosen for your texture and lifestyle, can help make everyday styling feel more manageable.

Be kind to wet hair

After washing, gently squeeze out excess water and detangle with care. Avoid rough towel-drying, and use heat thoughtfully when styling.

Bring your routine to the consultation

The products and tools you already use are useful context when choosing a cut or color. Share what works, what feels difficult, and how much time you want to spend styling.
