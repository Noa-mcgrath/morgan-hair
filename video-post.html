STYLING NOTES

Soft waves, made simple.

A few gentle steps for a relaxed, polished finish at home.

Styling photo to be added

Prep for an even finish

Start with dry hair and use the heat-protection product that works for your hair. Keep sections manageable so the finish feels consistent.

Work in soft sections

Shape each section away from the face, alternating direction as you move around the head. Leave the ends relaxed for a softer look.

Let the style settle

Allow the hair to cool before gently loosening the waves with your fingers. Finish lightly and adjust the routine to suit your texture.
